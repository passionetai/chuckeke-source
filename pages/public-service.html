Public service

Twelve years on the ground in Delta State.

Government

Two terms, one constituency.

Commissioner and Representative of Ndokwa Federal Constituency in the Delta State Oil Producing Areas Development Commission, 2011–2015. Then twice Senior Special Assistant to the Governor of Delta State — first on Political Matters, then on Community Development — between 2018 and 2023. In 2014, in collaboration with non-governmental organizations and eminent personalities, Chuck hosted an ethnic-nationality conference and rally at Cenotaph Park, Asaba, attracting federal appointees and traditional rulers from across the country.

Civil society & human rights

Thirty years of advocacy.

As Program Director of the Foundation for the Conservation of the Earth (FOCONE), Chuck represented civil society at the Voluntary Principles Initiative on Security and Human Rights. Plenaries in Toronto (2022) and London (2022, 2023). UN participation since the 6th UN Forum on Business and Human Rights in Geneva, 2017. Hosted UN Human Rights Day in Asaba, December 2019, and World Environment Day in Ikeja, Lagos, in collaboration with the National Youth Service Corps and the University of Lagos.

Community leadership

President, chair, patron.

President of Anioma Association of Massachusetts Inc., 2021–2023; Chairman of the Re-Organization Committee of the Igbo Organization of New England (IONE); member of the Igbo Defense Fund, Massachusetts; pioneer Patron of the Coalition of Ndokwa Peace Ambassadors (CONPA), founded in the gazebo of his family home in Asaba.
