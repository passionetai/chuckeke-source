Two entrepreneurship volumes, one feature film shot across six cities and two continents, and an inspirational publication in print and online. Read on, or jump to films · books · publishing. 

Films

##  Rainbow: Living Your Dream.

An action-packed intercontinental feature shot across Lagos and Asaba in Nigeria; Orlando, Cambridge, Canton and Boston in the United States. Inspired by the lives of Anderson Cooper, Van Jones and Oprah Winfrey. Two protagonists. One God-given dream apiece. Crime, faith, and a beauty pageant on the Lekki Barracuda Beach.
