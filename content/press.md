Ambassador (Rev.) Chukwudi "Chuck" Eke is a Nigerian-American statesman, author, filmmaker and ordained reverend evangelist based in Boston, Massachusetts, with his wife Caroline and their family. Born Omordi Chukwudi in Aboh, a riverine community of the ancient Aboh Kingdom in Delta State, Nigeria, he is a graduate of Harvard University (Class of 2005), a Fellow of the Chartered Institute of Administration, and a former Logistics Team Leader at Target Corporation in Boston. From 2011 to 2015 Chuck served two terms as Commissioner representing Ndokwa Federal Constituency in the Delta State Oil Producing Areas Development Commission under Governor Uduaghan; from 2018 to 2023 he was Senior Special Assistant to the Governor on Political Matters and on Community Development. He is the author of four books, including The Legacy of Atiku Abubakar on Nation-Building (2019), From Revelation to Revolution (2021), Breakthroughs Through Breaking the Rules (2024), and the forthcoming Davido: A Cultural Force for National Unity (July 4, 2026). He is Founder and Editor-in-Chief of Word Bank International Magazine, and executive producer of Rainbow: Living Your Dream, an intercontinental feature shot across six cities in Nigeria and the United States. In January 2026 he was appointed Eminent Peace Ambassador by the International Association of World Peace Advocates (IAWPA) and consecrated in Boston by Rev. Father Tom. He chairs the Rotary Foundation at the Rotary Club of Global Impact, Chicago.
