About

#  Ambassador (Rev.) Chukwudi Chuck Eke. 

Born Omordi Chukwudi in Aboh, a riverine community of the ancient Aboh Kingdom in mid-western Nigeria. Omordi for "Prince," Chukwudi for "God is alive." The penname came later, when literature and film called.

Early life

## From Aboh to America.

Chuck hails from Aboh in Delta State, Nigeria. His father's first name is Charles — a coincidence that quietly tracks across the page once you know it. He is, in his own phrase, "a man of many parts": author, administrator, scriptwriter, producer, director, songwriter, publisher, and motivational speaker; quick to learn and quicker to teach.

Harvard & education

## Class of 2005, Cambridge.

A graduate of Harvard University and a Fellow of the Chartered Institute of Administration. Briefly taught at the associate-degree Quincy College, Quincy, Massachusetts. Served as Logistics Team Leader at Target Corporation in Boston. The classroom and the boardroom both, before the cabinet table.

Government service

## Twelve years in Delta State.

Two-term Commissioner in the Delta State Oil Producing Areas Development Commission (DESOPADEC), 2011–2015, representing Ndokwa Federal Constituency under Governor Uduaghan. Twice Senior Special Assistant to the Governor — first on Political Matters, then on Community Development — between 2018 and 2023. [See the full timeline →](/public-service)

Ministry & faith

## Ordained reverend, Eminent Peace Ambassador.

Ordained Reverend Evangelist; Founder/CEO of _Word Bank International Magazine_ ; publisher of the _Wings of the Word_ devotional network. Appointed Eminent Peace Ambassador by the IAWPA on January 17, 2026, and consecrated in the Catholic parish at West Roxbury, Boston, by Rev. Father Tom.
